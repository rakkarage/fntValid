1 · How a character sits in its line

Every line of text is a box lineHeight pixels tall. The pen starts at the top-left of that box. Each glyph is a rectangle cut from the atlas PNG (x y width height) and drawn at pen + (xoffset, yoffset). After drawing, the pen moves right by xadvance.

Top of line: y = 0.
